# Re-derivation of open questions, 2026-09-15

Companion to `leads/rederive.json`. Method: every fact below was read from a URL opened on 2026-09-15; all 66 spans in the JSON were checked as exact substrings of `bench.review.fetch_text` output on 2026-09-15 and pass; sources that `fetch_text` cannot read (gzip-served renders, a POST-only API, a bot-blocked site, a JSON API that answers zero-result queries with HTTP 404) carry no spans and say so in their notes. Filings were read from IRS e-file XML on the GivingTuesday S3 mirror where present and otherwise from ProPublica's `full_text` render, which is served gzip-encoded and had to be decompressed locally; `fetch_text` returns raw gzip bytes for that endpoint, so amounts taken from it carry no checkable span. Greps of filings were case-insensitive (e-file names are upper case). Proposed signal ids (`metr.24`, `metr.25`, `apollo.16` to `apollo.18`, `irregular.15`, `palisade.14`, `securebio.24`, `caisi.17`, `euaio.14`, `ukaisi.15`) were the next free ids at write time; another session is adding rows in parallel, so re-check before applying. ## 8. UK AISI Alignment Project — partial

AISI's own pages establish that the £27m is not a single fund AISI holds. The Clarification Questions say "the Funding Agreement (GFA) will be between the funder(s) and the host organisation only" and "funds will be directly paid to the host organisation"; the How to Apply page says the DSIT/AISI agreement "is standard and not subject to negotiation" while "Other funders may issue different terms and conditions as part of their GFA"; for-profit applicants "may receive venture capital investment" rather than grants; and the example DSIT-AISI agreement makes the grant "pursuant to section 5 of the Science and Technologies Act 1965" with an "AISI Research Lead" as grant manager. Selection is AISI-run: Research Sponsors review expressions of interest and "a formal assessment panel, independent of the Research Sponsors" reviews full applications. So OpenAI's £5.6m funds grants under OpenAI's (or its administrator's) own agreement rather than passing through AISI's accounts, which bears on the F.9 pool clause in ukaisi.11 and ukaisi.12. What no page says is whether the Alignment Project team is organisationally separate from AISI's evaluation teams; the About page names Renaissance Philanthropy only as a backer, and the earlier launch post (aisi.gov.uk/blog/the-alignment-project) and DSIT's find-a-grant listing return 404. The separation question therefore stays partial.
