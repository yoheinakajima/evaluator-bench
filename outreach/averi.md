## Dissent on the card

- Lower: Access is at the floor; no lower notch exists. The public record supports 0: AVERI's only completed engagement queried Gemini 2.5 Flash-Lite, a model public since July 2025, inside an enclave that hid the weights from the auditors, and the report says the pilot addressed some but not all ways a developer could tamper with the evaluation. A nonpublic endpoint or pre-release checkpoint in the current pilots would meet anchor 1, and the enclave protocol is a documented route to deeper access.
